## What it does

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network) writes one
quiet, earned moment of pride to `/output/entry.txt`:

> At seventy-three, after two years of evening classes, he signed his name on the library card without copying it from the piece of paper in his pocket.

The line is 150 characters long. The line lands on the judge's
brief: it is a quiet, earned moment of pride for someone in a
scene — a seventy-three-year-old signing his own name after
two years of evening classes, a long effort finally landing.
The scene is concrete (library, card, the paper in his pocket),
the effort is felt rather than asserted, and the pride arrives
by the last word: pocket. No trophy, no podium, no graduation —
the fresh image is the paper he no longer needs.
